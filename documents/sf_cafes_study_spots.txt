Minerva Student Reviews — San Francisco Cafes & Study Spots
============================================================
Compiled from student Discord channels, group chats, and anonymous surveys (2024–2025)

---

Sightglass Coffee (SoMa, 270 7th St)
Rating: ★★★★★
"Honestly the GOAT study spot in SF. Big communal tables, outlets everywhere, and the wifi is rock solid — I've done 6-hour coding sessions here without a single drop. The cold brew is $6 but worth it. Gets busy after 2pm on weekdays though, so arrive early if you want a table."
— Class of 2026 student

"Second this. Sightglass SoMa is where half our cohort goes when we need to grind. Noise level is perfect — busy enough that you don't feel awkward on Zoom calls, but not so loud you can't concentrate. The espresso drinks are top tier. Only complaint: they close at 6pm which is annoying for night owls."
— M25, CS concentration

"Pro tip: the upstairs area has fewer people and more outlets. Also their wifi password changes weekly and is always on the little card at the counter."
— M26

---

Ritual Coffee Roasters (Valencia St, Mission)
Rating: ★★★★☆
"Great vibe for working but the wifi can be spotty during peak hours. I've gotten kicked off twice during Zoom calls. The coffee is amazing though — their single origin pour-overs are probably the best in the city. Tables are small so if you need to spread out with notebooks and a laptop, this isn't ideal."
— M25 student

"Love Ritual for solo work sessions. The baristas are super chill and don't give you the stink eye for camping with one $5 latte for 3 hours. But yeah, don't rely on the wifi for anything important. I always hotspot from my phone as backup."
— Class of 2027

---

Flywheel Coffee (Haight, 672 Stanyan St)
Rating: ★★★☆☆
"Cute spot right near Golden Gate Park. Good if you want a change of scenery but honestly the wifi is terrible. Like dial-up terrible. I tried to submit an assignment here once and ended up walking to the library. The pastries are good though."
— M26

"AVOID for study sessions. The tables wobble, there's maybe 2 outlets in the whole place, and the wifi is basically decorative. Fine for casual reading but not for anything that requires an internet connection."
— Class of 2026, Computational Sciences

---

Philz Coffee (Multiple locations)
Rating: ★★★★☆
"The one on 24th St in the Mission is my go-to. Wifi is reliable, there's usually a table available before noon, and they have those amazing mint mojito iced coffees. Prices are reasonable — $5-6 for a large. Not the quietest place though so bring headphones."
— M25

"Philz on Front St downtown is better for working. Less crowded, has a nice back area. I've written entire papers there. The staff remembers your order if you go regularly which is a nice touch."
— M27, Arts & Humanities

---

SF Public Library (Main Branch, Civic Center)
Rating: ★★★★★
"Free wifi, free printing (limited pages), dead quiet study rooms you can book online. This is the real secret weapon for Minerva students in SF. The 5th floor has individual study carrels with outlets and honestly it's better than any cafe. Zero distractions. The only downside is the neighborhood around Civic Center can feel sketchy, especially after dark."
— M26, Social Sciences

"The library saved my GPA first semester. Book a study room 2 days ahead — they fill up fast. Wifi is institutional grade so it never drops. Also they have free workshops on things like resume writing and data literacy which are actually decent."
— Class of 2025 alum

---

Blue Bottle Coffee (Various, try the one in Hayes Valley)
Rating: ★★★☆☆
"Overrated and overpriced for working. $7 for a latte and the tables are tiny. It's a great place for a 20-minute coffee break but terrible for a 3-hour study session. Very few outlets, wifi has a time limit (I think 2 hours?), and it's ALWAYS packed with tourists. Skip this if you're trying to work."
— M26

"Agree. Beautiful aesthetic, great coffee, awful study spot. Go to Sightglass instead."
— M25

---

PLACES TO AVOID FOR STUDYING:
- Starbucks on Market St: wifi requires purchase every 2 hours and resets, loud, no outlets
- Any cafe in Fisherman's Wharf area: tourist trap, overpriced, terrible wifi
- Peet's on Divisadero: closes too early, always a line, barely any seating
