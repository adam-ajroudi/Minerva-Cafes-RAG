Minerva Student Reviews — Seoul Cafes & Study Spots
=====================================================
Compiled from student KakaoTalk groups, Discord, and anonymous surveys (2024–2025)

---

Café Comma (Multiple locations, try Gangnam or Hongdae)
Rating: ★★★★★
"This is a Korean study cafe chain and it is INCREDIBLE. You pay by the hour (about ₩2,000-3,000/hour, so like $1.50-2.25 USD) and get unlimited coffee, tea, and sometimes snacks. The wifi is lightning fast, every seat has an outlet, and the atmosphere is dead quiet — everyone is studying. Open 24 hours. This saved my life during exam period."
— M26

"Cafe Comma is basically what every study cafe should be. The Hongdae location is huge — three floors, private booths, group study rooms. All included in the hourly rate. The coffee is surprisingly decent for being free/included. I went here almost every day during Seoul rotation."
— M25, Computational Sciences

---

Tom N Toms Coffee (Multiple locations)
Rating: ★★★★☆
"Korean coffee chain that's WAY better than Starbucks for studying. Most locations have a second floor that's quieter, outlets everywhere, and good wifi. An Americano is ₩4,500 (~$3.40) and they don't rush you out. The pretzel bread is addictive — try it. Open late (most close around midnight)."
— M27

"Tom N Toms is my go-to when I don't want to pay for a study cafe. The atmosphere varies by location — Sinchon one is great, Gangnam one is too crowded. Pro tip: the ones near universities (Sinchon, Ewha) are most study-friendly."
— Class of 2026

---

Study Cafe (스터디카페) — Various brands
Rating: ★★★★★
"If you haven't been to a Korean study cafe yet, GO. These are everywhere — literally every neighborhood has several. You pay ₩1,500-2,500/hour (~$1-2 USD), get a private booth with outlet and lamp, free drinks (coffee, tea, sometimes cocoa), and it's silent. Some have showers and sleeping pods. Korea takes studying seriously and it shows."
— M26

"The study cafe near our Minerva residence in Mapo-gu was open 24/7 and became my second home. ₩2,000/hour with free Americanos. I've never been more productive than during Seoul rotation and I credit these study cafes entirely."
— M25

---

Anthracite Coffee (Hannam-dong, near Itaewon)
Rating: ★★★★★
"The most beautiful cafe I've been to anywhere in the world. It's in a converted shoe factory — exposed brick, huge windows, industrial design. The wifi is solid, there are plenty of outlets, and the coffee is excellent (flat white ₩6,500 ~$5). Not cheap but worth it for the aesthetic and the focus it brings. I did my best work here."
— M26, Arts & Humanities

"Anthracite is where you go when you need inspiration AND productivity. The space is gorgeous, the music is always on point, and there's something about the atmosphere that just makes you want to create. Warning: it's popular for Instagram photos so weekends can be touristy."
— Class of 2027

---

Starbucks (Various — yes, really)
Rating: ★★★☆☆
"I know, I know — Starbucks in Korea? But here's the thing: Korean Starbucks is NOTHING like American Starbucks. Many locations have dedicated study floors with booths and outlets. The wifi is fast and free. And they have Korea-exclusive drinks that are actually amazing. The one near Gwanghwamun is three floors with a rooftop. Not my first choice but reliable when everything else is full."
— M26

---

PLACES TO AVOID FOR STUDYING IN SEOUL:
- Ediya Coffee in tourist areas: Too crowded, slow wifi, uncomfortable seats
- Any cafe in Myeongdong: Tourist trap, expensive, no wifi or time-limited
- Cheap cafes near Gangnam station: Way too noisy, designed for socializing not studying
- PC Bangs (internet cafes): Great internet but designed for gaming — very distracting and smoky
