Minerva Student Reviews — Best WiFi & Study Spots Ranked Across All Cities
=============================================================================
Compiled from end-of-rotation surveys and student Discord (2024–2025)

---

This document compiles responses from the annual "Best Study Spots" survey run by the Minerva Student Council. 87 students responded across all six rotation cities.

=== TOP 10 STUDY SPOTS BY WIFI SPEED (student-reported) ===

1. Café Comma (Seoul) — 150+ Mbps, ₩2,000/hour, 24hr
   "Fastest wifi of any study spot in any Minerva city. Not even close."

2. St. Oberholz (Berlin) — 100+ Mbps, free with coffee purchase
   "The unofficial Minerva Berlin campus. Everyone ends up here."

3. Sightglass Coffee SoMa (San Francisco) — 80-100 Mbps, free
   "Rock solid wifi, never drops even during peak hours."

4. LAB Training Center (Buenos Aires) — 80-100 Mbps, free with purchase
   "They literally post their wifi speed on the wall. Respect."

5. Louisa Coffee (Taipei) — 50-80 Mbps, free, some locations 24hr
   "Best value study cafe on the planet. $2 coffee, unlimited time."

6. Roastery Coffee House (Hyderabad) — 50+ Mbps, free with purchase
   "Only reliable fast wifi cafe in Hyderabad. Treasure it."

7. National Libraries (all cities) — varies, 30-80 Mbps, free
   "Every city's national/public library has decent wifi. SF Main Branch and Biblioteca Nacional in BA are the best."

8. Study Cafes / 스터디카페 (Seoul) — 80+ Mbps, ₩1,500-2,500/hour
   "Korea's study cafe concept should be exported to every country."

9. Bonanza Coffee (Berlin) — 40-60 Mbps, free
   "Not the fastest but very reliable. Great for deep work."

10. Anthracite Coffee (Seoul) — 50-70 Mbps, free
    "Beautiful space + good wifi = productivity heaven."

---

=== WORST WIFI EXPERIENCES (avoid for online work) ===

- Flywheel Coffee, SF: "Basically decorative wifi" — multiple students reported
- Einstein Kaffee, Berlin: "Drops every 20 minutes"
- Café Tortoni, Buenos Aires: "No wifi at all"
- Blue Bottle Hayes Valley, SF: "2-hour time limit on wifi"
- Ediya Coffee tourist locations, Seoul: "Slow and overcrowded"
- Starbucks Market St, SF: "Resets every 2 hours, requires repurchase"

=== BEST VALUE MEALS BY CITY (under $5 USD equivalent) ===

1. Hyderabad: Street dosa (₹30-80 = $0.36-0.96) — cheapest filling meal in any Minerva city
2. Seoul: Kimbap Cheonguk full meal (₩3,500-5,000 = $2.60-3.75)
3. Taipei: Night market dinner (NT$150-200 = $4.60-6.15 for multiple items)
4. Buenos Aires: Empanadas at El Sanjuanino (ARS $800 each = $0.80)
5. Berlin: Döner kebab (€4-7 = $4.30-7.50)
6. San Francisco: Señor Sisig burrito bowl ($12 — cheapest "filling" option but still expensive by comparison)

"If you rank the cities by how far a student budget goes for food, it's: Hyderabad > Seoul > Taipei > Buenos Aires > Berlin >>>>>> San Francisco. SF will destroy your budget if you eat out."
— M25, responding to the survey

---

=== LATE-NIGHT FOOD OPTIONS (after 10pm) ===

- Seoul: Study cafes with snacks (24hr), convenience stores (24hr), BBQ restaurants (until 2am)
- Taipei: Night markets (until midnight), 7-Eleven/FamilyMart (24hr)
- San Francisco: El Farolito (until 3am), limited late options otherwise
- Berlin: Döner shops (until 2-4am), Burgermeister (until midnight)
- Buenos Aires: Most restaurants serve dinner starting at 9pm (Argentine schedule), parrillas open until midnight
- Hyderabad: Ram Ki Bandi dosa cart (midnight-5am), Shah Ghouse (until 1am), street food until late
