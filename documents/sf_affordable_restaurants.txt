Minerva Student Reviews — San Francisco Affordable Restaurants
================================================================
Compiled from student group chats, Slack channels, and anonymous surveys (2024–2025)

---

Señor Sisig (Food truck, multiple locations + brick & mortar in Mission)
Rating: ★★★★★
"If you haven't tried Señor Sisig yet, what are you doing?? Filipino-Mexican fusion burritos for like $12 and they are MASSIVE. The California burrito with sisig is life-changing. There's usually a truck near Civic Center on weekday lunches. Best bang for your buck in the entire city."
— M26

"This is the best cheap meal in SF hands down. A burrito bowl feeds me for lunch AND dinner. The nachos are also insane. Get the garlic sauce on everything."
— Class of 2027

---

Tartine Manufactory (595 Alabama St, Mission)
Rating: ★★☆☆☆ (for affordability)
"INCREDIBLE food but absolutely not affordable. A sandwich is like $18, coffee is $7. This is a special occasion spot, not a weekly thing on a student budget. The morning buns are worth the splurge though — get there early before they sell out."
— M25

---

China Live (644 Broadway, Chinatown)
Rating: ★★★★☆
"The market restaurant downstairs (not the fancy upstairs one) has really solid lunch deals. Dim sum plates for $6-10, and you can get a full meal for under $15. The dan dan noodles are incredible. Way better than ordering delivery which kills your budget with fees."
— M26, Business concentration

---

El Farolito (Mission, 24th St)
Rating: ★★★★★
"THE late-night food spot for Minerva SF. Open until 3am, super burrito for $13, and it's genuinely one of the best burritos in the city. Cash only though — there's an ATM inside but it charges fees. The line gets long on Friday/Saturday nights but moves fast."
— M27

"I eat here at least twice a week. The quesadilla suiza is enormous and feeds two people for $14. Don't sleep on the horchata either. This place is a rite of passage for every Minerva student in SF."
— Class of 2026

"Only con: the seating area is kind of grungy and always packed. Most people get takeout. But for the price and quality? Unbeatable."
— M25

Kitchen Story (3499 16th St, Castro)
Rating: ★★★★☆
"Great brunch spot. The millionaire's bacon is famous for a reason. Prices are mid — entrees $15-20 — but portions are generous enough that you won't need to eat again for hours. Go on a weekday to avoid the 45-min weekend wait."
— M26

---

Dumpling Home (298 Valencia St)
Rating: ★★★★★
"Affordable dim sum in the Mission! Soup dumplings for $12 (8 pieces), pork buns for $8. A full meal with dumplings + a side is under $20 and you'll be stuffed. The quality is legit — these are some of the best XLB I've had outside of Shanghai."
— M26, originally from Taipei

"This place is always my recommendation for new Minerva students arriving in SF. Good food, fair prices, quick service. What more do you need?"
— M25

---

Bi-Rite Market (3639 18th St, Mission — groceries + prepared food)
Rating: ★★★★☆
"Not a restaurant but their deli counter has amazing sandwiches and salads for $10-13. Also this is where you get groceries if you're cooking — way better produce than Safeway, and their store-brand stuff is actually affordable. The ice cream shop next door (Bi-Rite Creamery) is legendary — $6 for a scoop but the salted caramel is worth every penny."
— M27

---

Tu Lan (8 6th St, SoMa)
Rating: ★★★★★
"Vietnamese food that will blow your mind for under $12. This place looks sketchy from the outside — it's in a rough part of SoMa — but the food is INSANE. The imperial rolls are famous. Julia Child used to eat here. Plates are huge. Cash only."
— M26, Art History

"Tu Lan is the ultimate Minerva hidden gem. Don't judge by the exterior. The combination plates are $10-12 and could feed a small army. Best pho I've had in the city."
— Class of 2026

BUDGET TIPS FROM MINERVA SF STUDENTS:
- Cook at the dorm! Splitting groceries with roommates saves hundreds per month
- Download the Too Good To Go app — restaurants sell leftover food for $4-6
- Happy hours: Zeitgeist (beer garden, $5 beers), Trick Dog ($10 cocktails on Mondays)
- Avoid: Doordash/UberEats — the fees double the price of everything
- Farmers market at Ferry Building on Saturdays has free samples (breakfast hack)
