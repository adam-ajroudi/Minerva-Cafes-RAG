Minerva Student Reviews — Berlin Affordable Restaurants
=========================================================
Compiled from student WhatsApp groups, Discord, and anonymous surveys (2024–2025)

---

Mustafa's Gemüse Kebap (Mehringdamm 32, Kreuzberg)
Rating: ★★★★★
"Yes, the line is insane (30-60 minutes on weekends). Yes, it's worth it. The döner kebap here is €6 and it's genuinely the best thing I've ever eaten for under €10. Grilled vegetables, perfectly seasoned meat, fresh bread. Go on a Tuesday afternoon and the line is only 10 minutes. This is a Berlin rite of passage."
— M26

"I was skeptical about waiting in line for a kebab but holy wow. The Gemüse Kebap with everything on it is life-changing. €6.50 and it's absolutely massive. Bring a friend to wait in line with and it goes fast."
— Class of 2027

---

Curry 36 (Mehringdamm 36, right next to Mustafa's)
Rating: ★★★★☆
"Currywurst for €3.50! This is the most Berlin thing you can eat. Quick, cheap, surprisingly good. Not a sit-down meal — you eat standing at a counter. Perfect for a late-night snack after studying. Pair it with fries and a drink and you're at €7 total."
— M25

---

Cocolo Ramen (Paul-Lincke-Ufer 39-40, Kreuzberg)
Rating: ★★★★★
"Best ramen in Berlin, and I've tried them all. The miso ramen is €12 and portions are big enough to fill you up completely. The space is tiny so expect to wait 15-20 min for a seat during dinner. But SO worth it. If you're craving comfort food during Berlin winter (and you WILL), this is the place."
— M26, originally from Seoul

"Cocolo is my comfort food go-to. The tantanmen ramen is incredible — spicy, rich, warming. It's not the cheapest option on this list but for restaurant-quality ramen, €12 is very fair. Cash only!"
— M25

Sahara Imbiss (Grünberger Str. 33, Friedrichshain)
Rating: ★★★★★
"Falafel plate for €5. Let me say that again. FIVE EUROS. Hummus, tabbouleh, pita, falafel — all fresh, all delicious. This is where I eat when I'm broke at the end of the month. The owner is super friendly and always gives you extra hummus."
— M27

"Best falafel in Friedrichshain, maybe all of Berlin. The shawarma plate is €7 and enormous. When people ask me how I survive on a student budget in Berlin, I say 'Sahara Imbiss three times a week.' Not even joking."
— Class of 2026

---

Markthalle Neun (Eisenbahnstraße 42/43, Kreuzberg)
Rating: ★★★★☆
"Street food market vibes. On Thursdays they do 'Street Food Thursday' with vendors from all over the world — plates range from €5-12. Great for a group outing with other Minerva students. On regular days it's more of a fancy grocery market. The kimchi stall is amazing."
— M26

"Thursday nights at Markthalle Neun are a Minerva tradition in Berlin. Each vendor has different food — Ethiopian, Japanese, Mexican, Turkish. You can sample a bunch of things for under €15. Gets very crowded after 7pm though."
— M25

---

Roamers (Pannierstraße 64, Neukölln)
Rating: ★★★☆☆ (value-wise)
"Beautiful cafe but kind of overpriced for what you get. An avocado toast is €11 and the portions are small. It's very Instagram-worthy though. I'd say go once for the experience but it's not a regular rotation spot on a student budget."
— M26

---

Burgermeister (Under the U-Bahn tracks, Schlesisches Tor)
Rating: ★★★★★
"Burgers under a train bridge!!! This place is iconic. A cheeseburger is €5.50 and it's one of the best burgers in the city. The meisterburger with jalapeños and bacon is €7. Fries are €3 and perfectly crispy. It's always crowded but the line moves fast. Don't leave Berlin without eating here."
— M27

"I've eaten here probably 30 times in my Berlin rotation. It never gets old. The burgers are juicy, the location is unforgettable, and the prices are student-friendly. There's basically no seating — you stand and eat — but that's the charm."
— Class of 2026

BUDGET TIPS FROM MINERVA BERLIN STUDENTS:
- Döner kebabs are €4-7 everywhere and are genuinely filling meals. Eat them.
- Turkish grocery stores (like Eurogida) have amazing prices on produce, bread, and cheese
- Berlin's tap water is safe and good — stop buying bottled water
- Late-night Späti (corner shops) have cheap beer (€1-2) and snacks
- Aldi and Lidl are your best friends for groceries — weekly shopping under €30 is doable
- AVOID eating near Alexanderplatz or Brandenburger Tor — tourist trap prices everywhere
