Minerva Student Reviews — Berlin Cafes & Study Spots
======================================================
Compiled from student Discord, WhatsApp groups, and anonymous surveys (2024–2025)

---

St. Oberholz (Rosenthaler Platz, Mitte)
Rating: ★★★★★
"The holy grail of coworking cafes in Berlin. Two floors, tons of outlets, blazing fast wifi, and nobody cares if you sit there for 8 hours. The upstairs coworking space is paid (€15/day) but the downstairs cafe area is free — just buy a coffee every few hours. Flat white is €4.50 which is totally reasonable for Berlin. This is where I wrote my entire capstone first draft."
— M26, Computational Sciences

"St. Oberholz is basically an unofficial Minerva campus. I've run into at least 3 other Minerva students here every time I go. The power outlets are at every table, wifi consistently hits 100mbps+, and the atmosphere is that perfect 'focused but not silent' level. Their breakfast bowls are also genuinely good and under €10."
— M25

"One warning: it gets PACKED after 11am. Like every freelancer and startup person in Berlin is here. If you want a guaranteed seat, arrive by 9:30. Also their card reader breaks sometimes so bring some cash."
— Class of 2027

---

Bonanza Coffee (Oderberger Str. 35, Prenzlauer Berg)
Rating: ★★★★☆
"Beautiful space, incredible coffee (they roast their own beans), and a nice quiet vibe for morning work sessions. Wifi is good — maybe not as fast as St. Oberholz but totally fine for regular work. The filter coffee is €3 and legitimately some of the best in the city. Seating is limited though — maybe 15 spots total?"
— M26

"Love Bonanza for solo deep work. No large groups, no loud conversations. The baristas are serious about coffee so if you're into that, ask them about their current roast. Just don't go expecting to stay past 5pm — they close early."
— M25, Natural Sciences

---

The Barn (Multiple locations, try Auguststraße in Mitte)
Rating: ★★★★☆
"Another specialty coffee spot with solid wifi. The Mitte location has more space than the Prenzlauer Berg one. Good for 2-3 hour sessions. Espresso is €3, pour-over is €4.50. They have a 'laptop-free' section which is annoying but also kind of respectful? The other tables are fair game. Outlets can be hard to find — bring a full battery."
— M27

---

Kaschk (Linienstraße 40, Mitte)
Rating: ★★★★☆
"This place is a bar at night but during the day it's a chill cafe with great wifi. They have a combo of specialty coffee and craft beer which is very Berlin. The wifi is fast and free, there are communal tables with outlets, and it's rarely crowded before 3pm. Day menu has decent sandwiches for €6-8. Highly recommend for afternoon work sessions."
— M26

---

Five Elephant (Reichenberger Str. 101, Kreuzberg)
Rating: ★★★★★
"Five Elephant in Kreuzberg is my all-time favorite Berlin study cafe. The cheesecake is LEGENDARY — probably the best in Berlin, no exaggeration. Wifi is strong, there are enough outlets, and the vibe is creative and focused. Flat white is €4. The space isn't huge so don't bring a group, but for solo work? Perfect."
— M25

"Can confirm the cheesecake is a religious experience. I go here every Saturday morning for my weekly review sessions. The baristas know my order now. If you're in Kreuzberg, this is the spot."
— Class of 2026

---

Staatsbibliothek (State Library, Potsdamer Str. 33)
Rating: ★★★★★
"Free, silent, has wifi, has outlets, open until 9pm on weekdays. If you need to LOCK IN and actually get work done, the Stabi is unbeatable. You need to register for a library card (free, bring your student ID) but after that it's the best study space in Berlin. The reading rooms are gorgeous and make you feel like a scholar."
— M26, Social Sciences

"Every Minerva student in Berlin should get a Stabi card on day one. I cannot stress this enough. It's quiet, the wifi is reliable, and you can book private study rooms. The cafe on the ground floor is also cheap — €2 for a coffee."
— M25

---

PLACES TO AVOID FOR STUDYING IN BERLIN:
- Einstein Kaffee (chain): Mediocre coffee, terrible wifi, tourist trap pricing near landmarks
- Any cafe on Friedrichstraße: Overpriced, noisy, zero outlets, designed for shoppers not workers
- Balzac Coffee: Generic chain, wifi cuts out after 1 hour, uncomfortable chairs
- Starbucks (anywhere): Why would you go to Starbucks in Berlin?? The local cafes are infinitely better
